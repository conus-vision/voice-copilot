Ukrainian: speak natural, everyday Ukrainian, the way one developer talks to another. Use Ukrainian words and spelling, not Russian or surzhyk ones ("перевіряємо", not "провіряємо"; "помилка", not "ошибка"). Say ordinary technical words in Ukrainian (розпакування, тіло запиту, потоковий); keep names of files, functions, commands and libraries in Latin script as written (config.py, pytest, zstandard). Everyday developer words such as тести, конфіг, коміт and гілка are fine.
